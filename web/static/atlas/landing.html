    What it does, and what it doesn't

    Explained, with the bias shown.

    News outlets don't all tell a story the same way. Stringer doesn't try to hide that or average it out. It shows you the split and the sources behind it.

    Stringer does

    • Read what each side published about the story.
    • Check every claim in the script against those articles.
    • Show who said what, and the words each side chose.
    • Leave out anything it can't match to an article.

    Stringer doesn't

    • Decide which side is right.
    • Promise you the whole truth. It can only be as good as the articles it read.
    • Hide that it's made by AI. Every video carries the label.

    Sometimes there's no real divide.

    After an earthquake or a flood, outlets mostly agree on what happened. Then the video says there's no real divide instead of inventing one. 7 of the last 31 stories were like that.

    The practical part

    Who can use it
    Anyone with a Columbia account, for now. You sign in with it.
    What it costs you
    Nothing. Each video costs the project about $0.66, and it can never cost more than $5.
    How long a video takes
    4 to 5 minutes. You can leave the page while Stringer works.
    How many videos
    10 a day for the whole site. Each person can start 3 an hour, and Stringer makes at most 3 at once.
    When something's wrong
    Every video has a Report link. One report hides it until we've checked it.
    Where the stories come from
    The live feed from Monitor the Situation. Stringer picks from its top 10.
